How we calculate

Clearday computes the US EPA Air Quality Index, using the 2024 PM2.5 breakpoints, from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) forecasts served by Open-Meteo. These are modelled estimates for an area roughly 11–40 km across, not readings from a monitor on your street.

Air-quality data from Open-Meteo (opens in new tab) (CC BY 4.0), based on forecasts from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS (opens in new tab)).
